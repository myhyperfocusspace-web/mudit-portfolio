01 / THE IDEA

An office rivalry with a twist.

A jealous employee tries to outshine a colleague, only for everyday grocery shopping to become the unexpected status symbol.

02 / CREATIVE STRATEGY

Make it relatable.

A story designed around Bengaluru working professionals, social comparison and recognizable office dynamics.

03 / STORY & CAMPAIGN THINKING

The setup, the reveal.

Set up the rivalry. Make the audience expect a status contest. Reveal the product as the real flex. Expandable campaign development can be added once approved assets exist.
